How Nafath approval works
  1. 1Tap Verify with Nafath. We send a request to the national Nafath platform.
  2. 2Open the Nafath app on this phone using your National ID or Iqama.
  3. 3Select the number displayed on this screen to approve the request.
  4. 4Come back to barq and tap Continue to finish your transfer.
Why we use Nafath

Nafath is the Kingdom’s unified national access platform. Verifying through it keeps your wallet secure and lets us lift your wallet limits for international transfers.
